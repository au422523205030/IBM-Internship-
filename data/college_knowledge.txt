Attendance Policy
Students should maintain the minimum attendance percentage required by their university and college regulations. The exact percentage, condonation rules, and shortage procedure should be verified with the college office because they can differ by institution.

Internal Assessment
Internal assessment may include tests, assignments, attendance, laboratory work, seminars, and other components defined by the department. Students should refer to their current department syllabus or academic regulations for exact mark distribution.

Leave and OD
Students needing leave or on-duty permission should submit the required request through the college's prescribed process and obtain approval from the appropriate faculty or department authority before the relevant absence whenever possible.

Exam Registration
Students should complete examination registration within the dates announced by the college/university. Late registration, fee details, and eligibility requirements should be checked in the latest official examination notice.

Project Submission
Final-year project students should follow their department's project schedule, including proposal, review, implementation, documentation, demonstration, and final submission dates. The latest department circular is the authoritative source for deadlines.

Library
Library users should follow the library's current borrowing limits, renewal rules, due dates, and fine policy. If a book is shown as unavailable, students should check the library catalogue or speak with the librarian.

Placement
Students should monitor official placement-cell announcements for company eligibility, registration deadlines, aptitude-test dates, interview schedules, and required documents. Eligibility can vary by company and batch.

Scholarship
Scholarship eligibility, income limits, required certificates, and submission dates vary by scholarship. Students should use the latest official scholarship notice and verify requirements with the scholarship/administration office.

Bonafide Certificate
A bonafide certificate is generally issued through the college's prescribed student-services or administration process. The exact application method and processing time should be confirmed with the office.

Academic Calendar
The academic calendar contains important dates such as semester start/end, examinations, holidays, and other academic events. Students should use the latest official calendar because dates can change.
